Purpose:
Detect late sector delivery during streaming/FMVs.

Why it matters:
FMV playback depends on sectors arriving every ~13.33ms.
Even brief delays can cause audio/video desync or stutter.

What to measure:
- Sector cadence
- Deadline misses
- Jitter
- Worst-case latency

Pass Criteria:
- Zero deadline misses during 30+ minutes playback
- Jitter < 2ms
- No burst delivery

Failure Symptoms:
- FMV stutter
- Audio crackle
- Random hangs
